Welcome to our website or application. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how they may be used on our website or application, and how users can manage their cookie preferences. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit them. They help websites operate correctly, remember certain preferences, and improve the browsing experience. Other technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, may perform similar functions.

Types of Cookies

3.1. Essential Cookies
Essential cookies are necessary for a website or application to function properly. They may support security, network management, accessibility, and the saving of privacy preferences. Where essential cookies are used, they are used to support the basic operation and security of our website or application.

3.2. Performance and Functionality Cookies
These cookies may help a website remember user preferences or previous interactions, such as language settings or login status. We will use these cookies only if the relevant functionality is implemented on our website or application.

3.3. Marketing Cookies
Marketing cookies may be used to track visitors across websites, build advertising profiles, or deliver personalized advertisements. Based on the information provided, our website or application does not display advertisements and does not use Facebook Pixel or advertising retargeting. We therefore do not intend to use marketing cookies for these purposes.

3.4. Analytics and Customization Cookies
Analytics cookies help website operators understand how visitors use their websites, including which pages are visited and how users interact with features. Based on the information provided, our website or application does not use analytics services such as Google Analytics. If analytics tools are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated as necessary.

3.5. Social Media Cookies
Social media cookies may be placed by social networking services when their features are integrated into a website, or when users interact with embedded social content. We do not intentionally use social media cookies for tracking purposes. If social media integrations are added in the future, the relevant cookie practices will be reviewed and disclosed where appropriate.

3.6. Third-Party Cookies
Third-party cookies are created by services operated by organizations other than the website owner. Based on the information currently provided, we do not use third-party advertising or analytics tracking services. However, certain website hosting, security, or embedded services may use cookies depending on their configuration. We will review third-party services used on our website or application and update this policy if necessary.

Do We Display Advertisements?

No. Based on the information provided, our website or application does not display advertisements or use advertising retargeting technologies. We do not use cookies to deliver targeted advertising through Facebook Pixel or similar advertising tools.

How to Manage Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies, remove existing cookies, or receive notifications when cookies are placed on your device. Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect certain website features or prevent some functions from working correctly.

Personal Information and Emails

Our website or application sends emails to users, as previously indicated. Cookies are separate from email communications. Sending emails does not necessarily require the use of cookies. If email links or other services use tracking technologies, those practices should be disclosed separately where applicable.

Privacy and Data Protection

We aim to handle any information collected through cookies responsibly and in accordance with applicable Ugandan data protection and privacy requirements. We will update this Cookie Policy if our use of cookies, analytics, advertising, or third-party services changes.

Changes to This Cookie Policy
