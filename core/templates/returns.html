WaziTrade — Return and Refund Policy
Last updated: 20 October 2026

Our commitment to customers

At WaziTrade, we want every customer to have a positive shopping experience. If you receive a damaged, defective, incorrect, or misdescribed product, or need to request an eligible return, we will work with you to find a fair solution. This Return and Refund Policy applies to purchases made through the WaziTrade online marketplace in Uganda. Nothing in this policy limits any consumer rights provided by applicable Ugandan law.

When can you return a product?

    You may request a return, exchange, or refund in the following circumstances: • Damaged on arrival: The product arrives physically damaged. • Defective product: The product does not function properly or has a defect covered by applicable law or warranty. • Wrong product delivered: You receive a different product, model, size, colour, or quantity from what you ordered. • Product not as described: The product materially differs from its description or advertised specifications. • Order cancellation: You qualify for cancellation under applicable law or an applicable WaziTrade cancellation policy. • Change of mind: You wish to return a product for personal reasons, subject to applicable cancellation rights and any additional change-of-mind return conditions published by WaziTrade. We may request reasonable information to verify a return request. This does not affect any legal rights you may have.

Return period

Please contact WaziTrade as soon as possible after receiving your order if there is a problem. For change-of-mind returns, WaziTrade's proposed standard policy is to accept requests made within seven days of delivery, subject to the product's eligibility and applicable law. Other return requests should be reported promptly. Any applicable statutory cancellation, warranty, or other legal deadline will take precedence over a shorter period stated in this policy. Some products may be excluded from voluntary change-of-mind returns because of their nature or applicable legal exceptions. Any such exclusions will be disclosed clearly and will not remove mandatory consumer rights.

Condition of returned products

Where reasonably possible, returned products should include: • The product and its supplied accessories. • Manuals, warranty documents, and other items supplied with the product. • Original packaging, where available. • Proof of purchase or other information that enables WaziTrade to verify the order. Original packaging is preferred but is not always mandatory. A missing box or opened packaging will not automatically disqualify a customer from a remedy required by law. Customers should not deliberately damage, alter, or misuse products they intend to return. Products damaged by customer misuse or unauthorised alterations may not qualify for a voluntary changeof-mind refund, subject to applicable law.

Can discounted products be returned?

Yes. Products purchased during sales, promotions, clearance events, or other discount campaigns may be returned or refunded under the same applicable eligibility rules as other products. A discount alone is not a reason to refuse a valid return or refund. Any specific, lawful limitation must be disclosed before purchase and cannot override statutory consumer rights.

Can I return a damaged or defective product?

Yes. If your product arrives damaged, is defective, or does not match its description, contact WaziTrade customer support. Please provide, where reasonably available: • Your order number. • A description of the problem. • Photographs or a short video showing the damage or defect. • Any relevant packaging or delivery information. Do not discard the product or its accessories before contacting support, where reasonably possible. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, the appropriate remedy may be a replacement, repair, refund, or another suitable solution.

Can I return a product without a receipt?

Yes. A printed receipt is not the only way to establish proof of purchase. WaziTrade may verify your purchase using: • Your order number. • Your WaziTrade account or order history. • A payment confirmation. • An electronic receipt or other reliable transaction record. Providing sufficient information helps us process your request efficiently.

Who pays for return shipping?

Return shipping costs depend on the reason for the return and applicable law. WaziTrade or the responsible seller will arrange or cover reasonable return costs where required by law or the applicable policy, including eligible cases involving an incorrect, defective, damaged-on-arrival, or materially misdescribed product. For eligible change-of-mind returns, customers may be responsible for direct return shipping costs where lawful and disclosed in advance. No return charge will be imposed contrary to applicable consumer protection law. Please contact customer support before sending a product back so that we can provide the correct return instructions.

How to request a return or refund

Follow these steps: Step 1: Contact WaziTrade. Reach us through the official website contact page, customer support email, or telephone number. Step 2: Provide your order details. Include your order number, the product name, the reason for the return, and any supporting evidence available. Step 3: Wait for instructions. We will review your request and explain the next steps, including whether the product must be returned, inspected, repaired, or replaced. Step 4: Return the product if required. Use the return method and destination provided by WaziTrade or the responsible seller. Step 5: Receive the outcome. We will communicate the decision and, where approved, arrange the applicable refund, replacement, or other remedy. Please do not send products to an address that has not been confirmed by WaziTrade or the responsible seller.

How are refunds processed?

Approved refunds will generally be returned through the original payment method where practicable. If this is not possible, WaziTrade may arrange another lawful refund method with the customer. The refund amount will reflect the remedy to which the customer is entitled. Any deduction must be lawful and properly applicable to the transaction. Where a statutory refund deadline applies, WaziTrade will comply with that deadline. Under the applicable provisions of Uganda's Electronic Transactions Act, certain cancellations require a refund within 30 days. The time taken for the funds to appear in your account may depend on your bank, mobile money provider, or payment service provider.

Will I be notified when my refund is completed?

Yes. WaziTrade will notify you when your refund has been processed using the contact information associated with your order, where the relevant communication channel is available. The notification may be sent by email, SMS, telephone, or another available communication method. Please note that confirmation of processing does not always mean that the money will appear immediately in your account. Your payment provider may require additional processing time.

When may a refund request be declined?

A request for a voluntary change-of-mind return may be declined where the product is not eligible under the applicable policy or a lawful exclusion applies. A refund may also be declined where reliable evidence establishes that the claim is fraudulent or that the reported damage was caused by customer misuse, where relevant to the claim. However, WaziTrade will not reject a valid statutory claim simply because a product was discounted, its packaging was opened, or a printed receipt is unavailable. If your request is declined, we will communicate the reason and any available next steps. You may raise a complaint if you disagree with the decision.

Exchanges and replacements

Where an exchange or replacement is appropriate, it will depend on product availability and the applicable legal requirements. If the same product is unavailable, WaziTrade will discuss the available options with you, which may include a refund where required or appropriate. A customer will not be required to accept store credit instead of a refund where the law requires a monetary refund.

Order cancellations and unavailable products

If a product becomes unavailable after an order is placed, WaziTrade or the responsible seller will inform the customer as required by law and arrange an appropriate solution. Where payment has been made for an order that cannot be fulfilled, the customer will receive a refund in accordance with applicable law. Customers who wish to cancel an order should contact WaziTrade promptly. Cancellation eligibility may depend on the order's status, whether delivery has occurred, the nature of the product, and applicable legal rights.

Complaints and unresolved refund requests

If you are dissatisfied with the handling of a return or refund, contact WaziTrade customer support and provide your order number and details of the issue. We will make reasonable efforts to investigate and resolve your complaint. You may also pursue any remedies available through the appropriate Ugandan consumer protection authorities or courts.
