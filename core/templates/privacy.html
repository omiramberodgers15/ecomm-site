What is the meaning of privacy policy?

A privacy policy is a formal statement published by an organization that describes how it collects, uses, stores, shares, and safeguards the personal information of its customers and website visitors. A comprehensive privacy policy typically sets out: The information collected, such as names, contact details, delivery addresses, and payment information The purpose of collection and how the information will be used The parties with whom information may be shared, such as delivery and payment service providers The security measures in place to protect the information The rights of individuals, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of their data Importance: A privacy policy demonstrates transparency and accountability, strengthens customer trust, and helps an organization meet its legal obligations. In Uganda, the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 requires organizations that handle personal data to do so lawfully and openly. For an online business, a clear privacy policy assures customers that their information is handled responsibly.

What types of data does WaziTrade collect from customers or potential customers?

Wazitrade collects only the information necessary to provide its products and services. This may include: Personal identification details: full name, telephone number, and email address. Delivery information: physical address and location details required to deliver orders. Order and transaction information: products purchased, order history, and payment confirmations. Payments are processed by trusted payment providers, and Wazitrade does not store PINs or full card details. Technical and usage information: device type, browser, IP address, and website activity, collected through cookies and similar technologies to improve the user experience. Communication records: inquiries, feedback, and complaints submitted through our website, phone, email, or social media channels. Marketing preferences: your consent choices for receiving promotional offers and updates. For potential customers, such as website visitors or those who contact us before purchasing, Wazitrade may collect basic contact details and website usage information so that we can respond to inquiries and improve our services.

Payment Security

Payments are processed through secure payment providers. WaziTrade does not store your complete payment card information. Transactions are encrypted to protect your personal and financial information.

What does it mean to protect your personal information?

Protecting personal information means keeping it safe from loss, misuse, unauthorized access, or disclosure. It involves collecting only what is necessary, using it only for stated purposes, restricting access to authorized persons, storing it securely, and deleting it when it is no longer needed.

How do you protect personal privacy?
